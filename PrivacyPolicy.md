# Privacy Policy for Box AI for Chrome

Last updated: August 11, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes the data handling practices of the Box AI for Chrome extension ("the extension").

## No Personal Data Collection by the Developer

This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer or any third-party servers controlled by the developer. All data processing occurs locally within your browser or is transmitted directly to the Box.com API.

## Information Handled by the Extension

The extension handles the following information to function:

*   **Selected Text:** The text you highlight on a webpage is accessed by the extension to be sent to the Box AI API for processing. This data is not stored by the extension after the API call is complete.
*   **User Identity (via Chrome):** The extension uses the `identity` permission to authenticate you with your Box.com account. This is a standard Chrome feature that allows the extension to act on your behalf with Box.com. Your identity information is managed by Chrome and Box.com and is not accessed or stored by the developer.
*   **Extension Settings:** The extension uses the `storage` permission to save your settings (such as API keys or preferences) locally on your computer. This data is not transmitted to the developer.
*   **Web Page Content:** The extension requires permission to access web page content to perform its primary function of capturing selected text.

## Data Sent to Third Parties

The only data transmitted to a third party is the text you select, which is sent directly to the Box AI API. The use of this data is governed by the [Box Privacy Policy](https://www.box.com/legal/privacypolicy). We strongly recommend you review their policy to understand how your data is handled.

## Data Security

The extension relies on the security measures of your browser and the Box.com platform. As the developer does not store your data, the security of your data is primarily in your control and subject to the policies of Google and Box.

## Your Choices

You can control the extension's permissions at any time through your Chrome browser's settings. You can also uninstall the extension to remove it completely.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the extension's functionality or to comply with legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page.

## Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us using the contact information provided on the extension's Chrome Web Store page.
